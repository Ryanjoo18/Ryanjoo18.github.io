I am an undergraduate at the National University of Singapore (NUS), studying mathematics and computer science.

## Projects
- **[NUS Course Selection & Timetable Optimiser](https://github.com/Ryanjoo18/nus-course-timetable-optimizer)**:
A Python tool that models course selection and timetable generation as a constraint satisfaction problem using Google OR-Tools. It accounts for academic eligibility, prerequisites, semester availability and scheduling conflicts to generate feasible course combinations and timetables.
- **[WOTB Tank Recommendation System](https://wotb-tank-recommender.streamlit.app/)**:
A content-based recommendation system for World of Tanks Blitz, using cosine similarity and K-means clustering to identify similar tanks and produce top-10 recommendations. [Source code](https://github.com/Ryanjoo18/wotb-tank-recommender/blob/master/main.ipynb).
- **[NUS Mathematics Interactive Course Map](https://ryanjoo18.github.io/nus-math-map/)**: 
An interactive map of prerequisites for mathematics courses at NUS. Course data is obtained from the [NUSMods API](https://api.nusmods.com/v2/). [Source code](https://github.com/Ryanjoo18/nus-math-map).
- **[Different Sizes of Infinity]({{ '/assets/pdfs/infinity.pdf' | relative_url }})**:
A poster introducing set cardinality and different sizes of infinity, prepared during high school.
